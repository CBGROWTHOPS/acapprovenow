Frequently asked

Questions we get a lot

How fast can you send someone out?

For emergencies (no heat, no AC in extreme weather), we typically match you with a pro within 15 minutes and get someone on-site same day. For scheduled work, most appointments are available within 1-3 business days.

Is the quote really free?

Yes. Estimates are always free — no obligation to book. You'll get transparent pricing before any work starts.

What if I have bad credit?

Our financing partners work with all credit profiles, including scores below 600. Approval decisions are based on more than just your credit score. You can check your options with no impact on your score.

Do you serve my area?

We match customers with vetted local HVAC pros in all 50 states. Enter your ZIP in the form above and we'll confirm coverage instantly.

Are the technicians licensed?

Every HVAC pro in our network is licensed and insured in the state they serve. We verify credentials before any tech is added to the network.

What brands do you install?

Our network installs and services all major brands — Trane, Carrier, Lennox, Rheem, Goodman, Bryant, American Standard, and more. Your matched pro will recommend the best fit for your home and budget.
