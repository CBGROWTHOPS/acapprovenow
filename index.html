Why it matters

What's hiding in your ducts & vents right now

Air quality

Air ducts

Most homes down here have never had the ductwork cleaned once. Years of dust, pet dander, drywall debris and mold spores sit in those ducts — and get blown through your house every single time the AC kicks on. If anyone in the family has allergies, asthma, or wakes up congested, this is often the reason.

Fire hazard

Dryer vents

Your dryer vent is the bigger problem. Lint buildup is the leading cause of household dryer fires, and a clogged vent adds time and money to every single load you run. If your clothes are still damp after a full cycle, or the dryer is hot to the touch, you're overdue.

Things people ask us

Is the estimate really free?

Yes. We come out, look at the job, and give you a real number on the spot. If you don't want to move forward, that's fine — the estimate costs you nothing.

How fast can you get here?

Same-day in most cases. Call us at 305-783-3669 — if we can get a technician to you today, we will.

Why do you push dryer vent cleaning so hard?

Lint buildup in dryer vents is the leading cause of household dryer fires. A clogged vent also makes every load take longer and cost more to run. It's a 30-minute job that pays for itself in reduced dryer time and eliminated fire risk.

Do you service appliances?

No — heating, cooling and air quality only. If your dryer itself is broken (not the vent), you'll want an appliance repair company.

What brands do you work on?

All the major HVAC brands — Carrier, Trane, Rheem, Ruud, Goodman, Lennox, York, Amana, Tempstar, American Standard, plus mini-splits and heat pumps.
